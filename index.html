📌 Faire Info

  • Chenango County Fairgrounds, 168 E Main St, Norwich, NY.
  • Gates 10 AM–6 PM, rain or shine.
  • Adult admission $17.
  • Kids 5 and under free. Public listings conflict on the 6–13 price, so verify at the gate/Facebook before relying on a number.
  • Firefighters, police, EMS & nurses: $10 with valid agency ID.
  • Veterans / active duty receive a special discount with ID.
  • Free parking; re-entry with stamp.
  • Many vendors may be cash-only; no ATM on site.
  • SCA event listing says no untied weapons except performers while performing.
