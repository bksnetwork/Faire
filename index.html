📌 Faire Info

  • Gates: 10 AM–6 PM, rain or shine.
  • Adult admission: $17. Children 6–13: $5; 5 and under free.
  • Firefighters, police, EMS, and nurses: $10 with valid agency ID.
  • Veterans / active duty: special discount with valid ID.
  • Parking is free. Re-entry permitted with stamp.
  • Gate accepts credit cards; many vendors may be cash-only. No ATM on site.
  • SCA event listing notes service animals only / no pets and tied weapons only, except performers while performing.
